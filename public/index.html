How to play CHASE.EXE

CHASE.EXE is a free online two-player chase game. One player runs while the other hunts. Create a private room and share its code, or use Find a Random Player. Avoid getting tagged until the 90-second round ends.

💻 Laptop and desktop

  • Move with W A S D or the ↑ ← ↓ → arrow keys.
  • Hold Shift to sprint. Sprint uses stamina; let go to recover it.
  • Select Fullscreen for a larger game view. Move around trees, logs and ruins to evade your rival.

📱 Mobile phone and tablet

  • Rotate your phone to landscape for the best view.
  • Drag the virtual joystick at the bottom-left to move in any direction.
  • Press and hold Sprint at the bottom-right to run faster. Release to save stamina.
  • If prompted, tap the landscape or fullscreen button. Keep both thumbs inside the game controls while playing.

🎮 Start an online match

  • Accept the player profile notice, choose a callsign, and save your profile.
  • Choose Find Match to look for a random opponent, or create a private room and share the invite code.
  • The runner must survive until the timer ends. The hunter tries to tag the runner before time runs out.
  • Use Copy Invite to share a room link with a friend. Both players need an internet connection.
Tip: Sprint only when you need to escape or close the gap. Your camera follows your character, so use the minimap to keep track of the arena.
